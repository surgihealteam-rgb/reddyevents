06 — FAQ

Questions, answered.

What types of events do you manage?

Weddings, engagements, receptions, haldi and mehendi functions, birthdays, anniversaries, baby showers, corporate conferences, annual days, launches, award nights, offsites, brand activations and customised celebrations.

Do you provide décor and production?

Yes. We can coordinate décor, stage design, lighting, sound, AV, fabrication, entertainment and other production requirements as part of an end-to-end event plan.

Can you work with my preferred vendors?

Absolutely. We can coordinate your existing vendors or recommend and manage suitable partners based on the event brief.

How do I get a quotation?

Call or WhatsApp 94404 83011 with your event type, preferred date, guest count and venue if known. We will take it from there.
